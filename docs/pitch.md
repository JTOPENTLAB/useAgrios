# AgriFlow — Investor Pitch

**From Farm to Money. The Financial Operating System for African Agriculture.**

---

## 1. Vision

Build the financial rails and trade graph for African agriculture.
Every kilo of produce that moves in Africa should move through AgriFlow.

## 2. Problem

African agriculture is $300B+ — and almost none of it flows through structured digital rails.
- Farmers lose 30–40% of margin to middlemen.
- Buyers have no reliable way to source verified supply.
- Payments are delayed, disputed, or lost.
- Credit is inaccessible to the people who actually grow food.
- There is no data layer — so no price discovery, no forecasting, no trust signals.

## 3. Solution

AgriFlow is an all-in-one platform that combines:
- **Marketplace** — structured listings, verified farmers, escrow-protected orders
- **Wallet** — ledger-accurate balances, instant settlement, payout rails
- **Escrow** — funds locked until delivery confirmed; disputes resolved by AgriFlow
- **Logistics** — auto-matched transporters, proof-of-delivery, tracked routes
- **Credit** — on-platform credit scoring, instant loans against future GMV
- **Intelligence** — live prices, demand, weather, AI recommendations
- **Content** — AI-generated viral video scripts for organic growth

## 4. Product (live today)

- 4 role-gated portals (Farmer, Buyer, Logistics, Admin)
- Escrow-first order flow with 5% take-rate
- Wallet with double-entry ledger
- AI: Claude-powered price recommender + video script generator
- Full loan lifecycle: apply → credit score → approve → disburse → repay
- Referral engine (invite → first-order bonus)
